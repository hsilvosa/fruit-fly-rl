# Completion verification for autonomous large-room navigation

Successful navigation remains unproven. This protocol prepares the evidence needed to finish the active task. It starts no training or evaluation and does not select a checkpoint.

A candidate must first reach at least seven of the eight reused original-large development goals. Those rooms have informed corrections, so their score is development evidence. A further 32-room validation batch is planned before final selection; its threshold is 26 goals. This additional validation is also selection data, not the independent final test. Historical exposure records and disjoint geometry must be audited before invoking it.

One candidate and its exact checkpoint and runtime hashes must then be frozen for a single independent final measurement. An existing unclaimed 64-room original-large pool is available; its frozen manifest and exposure ledger are recorded privately. It must pass the existing test-exposure guard and remain disjoint from optimization and previously exposed geometry. This document does not consume it.

The final point target is at least 80% success: at least 52 of 64 goals. Success, collisions, timeouts and the Wilson 95% interval must all be reported. This is a point target, not a claim that the interval lower bound exceeds 80% or that every possible room is solved. A final outcome must not select another checkpoint or trigger a retry on the same pool. A failed final remains recorded and exposed.

All flights use original generated poses and headings, coordinated dynamics and the unchanged large profile. The policy consumes activity of the complete annotated connectome. The teacher and hidden reference route are absent at inference. Checkpoint loading must preserve weights, and original aliases must retain their hashes.

Completion also requires an inspected interactive flight, working reset/new-room controls, a documented launcher and updated results and roadmap. Interface tests, static sensor comparisons and guided arrivals alone cannot satisfy the navigation target. No biological advantage will be inferred from successful navigation.
