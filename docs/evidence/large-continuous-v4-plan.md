# Continuous large-room adaptation

The bounded run was launched on 2026-10-03. Its PID, start time, configuration hash and runner hash are recorded in `private/large-continuous-v4-launch.json`; logs and status are under `runs/training/large-continuous-v4`. The full suite passed 150 tests, followed by six targeted runner tests including two new checks of the corrected budget and final-test gate. Results remain pending.

The goal remains at least 80% success in the unchanged original `large` target: a 48 by 48 by 16 room with 112 collision boxes and five partitions. This result is not yet established.

The previous adaptation stopped after 98,304 saved training transitions. Its 1,024-decision chunks discarded unfinished episodes before the minimum 1,200-decision timeout. This prevented those training episodes from receiving a timeout penalty. The corrected implementation retains the live worlds, brain activity, PPO observations and episode starts, optimizer, and random streams between chunks. Practice may open a stage gate, but the new profile takes effect only at the next natural episode reset. Evaluation uses separate environments, and its random seeds cannot replace the training random stream.

The corrected run uses the remaining 425,984 training transitions: 212,992 per training seed, in 26 chunks of 8,192. Combined with the interrupted run, the substantive training budget is 524,288 transitions. Both runs start from the original successful single-wall policy and its Adam state. They are warm adaptation runs with distinct training seeds, rather than fresh policy initializations. Interrupted-run checkpoints remain preserved as evidence.

The stages remain `gate-near`, `gate-long`, `gate-two`, `passages-wide`, `large-wide`, `large-narrow`, and `large`. Both eight-episode practice batches require seven successes before progression. Practice adapts training and is not independent generalization evidence. The fixed large target is validated every fourth chunk and at the last chunk; only validation selects the checkpoint. One reserved final assessment is permitted only if validation success reaches 85%. At least 52 successes in 64 final episodes are needed to reach the 80% point-estimate target; the Wilson interval, collisions and timeouts must also be reported.

The new suite excludes all nine earlier suites. There are 128 training seeds, including 16 withheld from optimization for practice, 32 validation seeds and 64 reserved final-test seeds. Original aliases and the warm-start files are checked with SHA-256 and never promoted or overwritten.

Regression tests use real flight physics to show a timeout at decision 1,200 across a 1,024/512 chunk boundary, including the terminal penalty and independent brain reset. Another test shows that practice advancement leaves an ongoing episode intact and changes the profile at its next natural reset. A CUDA check uses the full graph for 128 separate verification transitions with two deliberately small optimizer updates, finite losses, the same live optimizer, and checkpoint reload. Its rollout size is 64 only for verification; production remains 512. These checks establish continuity, not navigation performance. Saved checkpoints remain model archives, not full live-episode recovery snapshots.
